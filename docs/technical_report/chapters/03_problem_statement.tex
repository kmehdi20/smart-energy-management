\chapter{Problem Statement}
\label{ch:problem}

\section{Current Challenges}

Small buildings with rooftop PV installations and battery storage face several interconnected energy management challenges:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Lack of visibility:} Building occupants and operators have no real-time insight into energy flows---how much PV energy is being consumed directly, how much is stored, and how much is wasted.

    \item \textbf{Temporal mismatch:} PV generation peaks between 10:00 and 15:00, while residential consumption peaks between 18:00 and 23:00. This fundamental mismatch limits the direct use of solar energy.

    \item \textbf{Reactive grid usage:} Without forecasting, conventional systems draw from the grid during peak tariff hours even when earlier PV surplus could have been stored for later use.

    \item \textbf{Suboptimal battery dispatch:} Rule-based battery management does not consider future conditions, leading to premature full charge (missing afternoon surplus) or unnecessary grid imports.

    \item \textbf{Inflexible load scheduling:} Loads such as water heaters, washing machines, and water pumps have flexible operating windows but are typically run at fixed times without regard to PV availability.

    \item \textbf{No anomaly awareness:} Equipment malfunctions, unusual consumption patterns, or PV production drops go undetected until the monthly electricity bill reveals the problem.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Research Questions}

This project addresses the following questions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Can machine learning models accurately forecast short-term energy consumption and PV production for a Moroccan residential building?
    \item Does MILP-based optimization provide measurable improvements over rule-based energy management?
    \item How much can PV self-consumption and grid independence be improved through intelligent battery dispatch and flexible load scheduling?
    \item What are the economic and environmental benefits of an AI-based EMS in the Moroccan context?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scope}

The project scope is defined as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Building type:} Small residential or commercial building (single-phase, $\leq$10~kW peak load).
    \item \textbf{PV system:} 4~kWp rooftop installation.
    \item \textbf{Storage:} 5.12~kWh LiFePO4 battery (48~V / 100~Ah).
    \item \textbf{Grid connection:} Import only (no feed-in / net metering).
    \item \textbf{Optimization horizon:} Day-ahead (24 hours, 15-minute resolution).
    \item \textbf{Data:} Synthetic simulation data with provision for real sensor data.
    \item \textbf{Location:} Meknès, Morocco (latitude 33.9°N).
\end{itemize}
