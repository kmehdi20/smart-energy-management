\section{Emissions Factor}

Morocco's electricity grid relies on a mix of coal, natural gas, wind, solar, and hydroelectric generation. The grid emissions factor used in this analysis is 0.65~kg~CO$_2$/kWh, based on IEA country-level data for Morocco \cite{iea2024}. This value is subject to annual revision as the renewable share in Morocco's generation mix increases.

\section{CO$_2$ Avoided by PV}

Without the PV system, the building would import all 10,873~kWh from the grid, producing:

\begin{equation}
    \text{CO}_2^{\text{no PV}} = 10{,}873 \times 0.65 = 7{,}067 \text{ kg/year}
\end{equation}

The PV system avoids:

\begin{equation}
    \Delta\text{CO}_2^{\text{PV}} = 7{,}067 - 4{,}139 = 2{,}928 \text{ kg/year}
\end{equation}

This represents a 41.4\% reduction in CO$_2$ emissions from building electricity consumption.

\section{Additional CO$_2$ Avoided by Optimization}

The MILP optimizer further reduces grid imports by 22.5\% compared to the rule-based baseline (extrapolated from the 14-day summer comparison). The additional CO$_2$ reduction is:

\begin{equation}
    \Delta\text{CO}_2^{\text{EMS}} = 6{,}367 \times 0.225 \times 0.65 = 931 \text{ kg/year (estimated)}
\end{equation}
